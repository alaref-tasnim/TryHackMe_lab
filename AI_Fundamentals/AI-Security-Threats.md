good AI security hygiene:

1. Securing AI Models 
   * threats: prompt injection, model theft, privacy leakage 
   * Solution: Role Based Access control & Multi-Factor Authentication 
  
2. Privacy Protection:
   * Threat: Training data frequently contains sensitive information,
   * Solution: audited, minimised, and encrypted.

3. Model Monitoring:
    * Explainability tools like SHAP and LIME
    * SHAP and LIME are diagnostic tools that open the "black box" of AI models to show humans why an algorithm made a specific decision.
